Bhuvan Rai

Hi, I am Bhuvan. A boy from Delhi, India with a ton of dreams. I am making this site to document everything about myself and my journey, all the cool things I did, how I failed and how I stood up. Everything!

I was born in Delhi but my grandparents were originally from Punjab, India. Been living in Delhi ever since, and safe to say it is not that bad. I love to explore stuff, and I sure did explore it all, including singing, guitar, cooking, reading, and especially coding. Though I never fully reached my potential until after high school, mainly due to my family's beliefs. My family was not as supportive as most other teens might have gotten, but I still learnt to rule my own life.

Timeline

Phase 1: 2024-2026 , the so called "Golden period"

Finished middle school with all A grades or above. I thought to myself life is good, what else could go wrong. Then entered my life the thing forced on most Indian teens of my age, entrance exams. Before I could even think of what actually interests me, I was forced to choose one of three paths: Science, Commerce, or Arts.

You choose commerce or arts and you are insulted for the rest of your life. Choose science and you become the good talk in every family gathering.

Luckily for me, I always wanted science, not by force, but because I genuinely wanted to make something cool with computers. Skipping through two years of insane rat race grinding for my JEE examination comes the best part of my life ever.

Phase 2: pre college, my ACTUAL Golden period

Being real, my school never taught useful coding. During high school I was stuck making and learning boring, repetitive menu-driven Python programs. After school I had freedom to discover anything, bound by no syllabus or exams, and I fully invested into my biggest hobby, making computer stuff. That is when I found Hack Club, an organization built by teens, for teens. Finding it for me was the equivalent of finding gold.

I learnt day and night. The same guy who did not know what GitHub was had made 4+ projects in the same month in different fields, including my own 3D model, firmware and PCB for a macropad, a CLI sorter tool, a KNN-based Python drawn number guesser, and an OS-based personal website.

I also went to coding events and competitions to meet new people outside my school friends for once.

Phase 3: college, work in progress

I did not get my dream college, but my college is not half bad. The only thing I hate is how different I am from everyone, because I love to make stuff, while others are just focused on bunking classes to do nothing.

I am trying to learn more so I can resume building cool stuff again, so this phase of my life will be the biggest and most interesting one for me.

With regards, Bhuvan Rai
